New preprint: [High-fidelity Raman matterwave control by composite biased rotations](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.5.043094). Published in *Physical Review Research* **5**, 043094 (27 Oct 2023). In the intermediate-detuning regime of Raman control, light shifts bias every spinor rotation by a fixed angle. We show that composite sequences of such biased rotations give precise, nanosecond-scale control of hyperfine matter waves, even under strongly inhomogeneous illumination and for several Zeeman pseudo-spins at once.
