[Ultrafast quantum control of atomic excited states via interferometric two-photon Rabi oscillations](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-024-01585-x). Published in *Communications Physics* **7**, 98 (16 Mar 2024). Ultrafast laser pulses drive two-photon Rabi oscillations between excited states of argon at terahertz Rabi frequencies, far beyond what conventional coherent control reaches. Using quantum-path interferometry, the amplitude and phase of the transition dipoles are measured and steered through the intensity and polarization of the driving laser, enabling femtosecond population transfer and a controlled geometric phase.
