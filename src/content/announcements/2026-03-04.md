New preprint: [Constructing arbitrary coherent rearrangements in optical lattices](https://doi.org/10.1103/jsr3-2r5t). Published in *PRX Quantum* **7**, 043021 (30 Oct 2026). We show how to implement any single-particle unitary on atoms in an optical superlattice by combining global tunnelling with site-resolved phase shifts, in analogy with the Clements decomposition of linear optics. In two dimensions this gives all-to-all atom rearrangement with a circuit depth that grows sublinearly with the atom number.
