New preprint: [Realizing the Emery model in optical lattices for quantum simulation of cuprates and nickelates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11037), with Hannah Lange, Annabelle Bohrdt and colleagues. We propose a way to realize the three-band Emery model of the copper-oxide planes with ultracold atoms, using a two-dimensional optical lattice with a superimposed pattern of repulsive potentials. The scheme reaches parameter regimes relevant to cuprates and infinite-layer nickelates at system sizes that are out of reach for current numerics.
