New preprint: [Realizing the Emery model in optical lattices for quantum simulation of cuprates and nickelates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11037), with Hannah Lange, Annabelle Bohrdt and colleagues.

AI summary: A two-dimensional optical lattice with a superimposed pattern of repulsive potentials realizes the three-band Emery model of the copper-oxide planes in regimes relevant to cuprates and infinite-layer nickelates.
