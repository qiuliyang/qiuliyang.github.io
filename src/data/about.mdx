I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics](https://www.mpq.mpg.de/) in Garching, where I work on [FermiQP](https://www.quantum-munich.de/387220/fermiqp), a fermionic quantum processor built around a quantum gas microscope. We use ultracold lithium atoms in optical lattices to simulate the Fermi-Hubbard model and to explore programmable quantum dynamics of fermions.

My current interests are the physics of doped Mott insulators (magnetic polarons, stripes, kinetic magnetism and pairing), extensions of the single-band Hubbard model such as the three-band Emery model, and coherent control of atoms in optical superlattices. On the numerical side I use tensor-network (DMRG, finite-temperature MPS) and quantum Monte Carlo methods to connect microscope snapshots with theory.

Before moving to Munich I did my PhD with Saijun Wu at Fudan University, developing nanosecond and picosecond optical pulse control of atomic matter waves, composite Raman pulses, and holographic imaging of cold atoms.
